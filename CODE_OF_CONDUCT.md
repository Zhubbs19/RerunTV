# Code of Conduct

RerunTV follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link; this page is the short version.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good faith, give and accept feedback
gracefully, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing other people's private information, and sexualized language or attention are not
acceptable anywhere in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem privately, open a private security advisory titled "Conduct report" from the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**); only the maintainer can see it. Please
include links to what happened. All reports are reviewed and handled fairly and with discretion.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or restrict or block participants who
violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
