CH 03 Now airing

Your own media library, on the air.

RerunTV turns folders of your own video into cable channels. Each channel has been running since the moment you made it, so when you tune in, you land mid-show, with a program guide, commercial breaks and the look of an old cable box.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

{{RELEASE_NOTE}} Beta software. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows will warn you the first time; here's how to check the download. Free and open source (MIT).

Channels are never restarted

When you make a channel, it gets an anchor time. Whenever you tune in, RerunTV works out what would be playing right now if that folder had been looping ever since. Nothing runs in the background: it's arithmetic against the clock, so you can close the app for a week and the schedule is still right when you come back.

What's on

ChannelProgram
02Program guide

A time-grid guide with a live “now” line, channel icons, filters, seven-day search and reminders that can tune the set for you.

04Time machine

Rewind or fast-forward the whole lineup, or jump to a date. Pick any show in the guide and it plays from its beginning.

05Commercial breaks

Bumpers and ad breaks from your own folders, timed per channel or per block, and moved to the fades to black where shows were cut for commercials.

07Dayparting

Cartoons in the morning, something else overnight: schedule blocks swap a channel's folder by the hour, and holiday blocks take over for a date range.

09Cable-box look

A mid-2000s cable-box layout in two skins, plus themes spanning decades of TV, CRT scanlines, channel-change static and a logo bug in the corner.

11Media library

A catalogue of your files, program details from .nfo files and tags, channel presets and smart channels built from a saved search.

13Households & DVR

Per-person favorites, reminders and resume points, recordings from the guide, and a PIN parental lock.

15Remotes & TV mode

Run it full screen on a TV over HDMI with a remote, or pair a phone on the same Wi-Fi as a remote and setup screen.

17Plug-ins

Extra channels and overlays: a channel mosaic, local weather, captions, breaking-news overlays, metadata from TMDB or TVmaze, or write your own.

Security

The threat model, the LAN pairing design, and how to report a problem.

Photosensitivity. RerunTV imitates old television, and some effects flicker or flash (scanline flicker, channel-change static, power animations, alert overlays). They can be turned down or off in Setup → Picture & Sound and on the Plug-ins page.

How it's built. RerunTV was written almost entirely by an AI coding agent under the project owner's direction and hasn't had an independent code review or security audit. More about that.
